ⓘ readme.txt
_□×

These are toys. Every score is produced by weights somebody made up on purpose, and every app will happily tell you something confident about your life based on nine sliders. That is the joke.

What they are actually good for: making a vague 2am feeling specific enough to say out loud. The number does not matter. The sentence you say afterward does.

No data leaves your device. There is no server, no analytics, no account. The visitor counter is a lie computed from localStorage, as god intended.
